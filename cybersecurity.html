Our Approach

We start with a comprehensive Risk & Vulnerability Assessment—external pen tests, network audits, and code reviews mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK® framework. Based on findings, we implement a defense-in-depth architecture: Zero Trust zones, micro-segmentation, and next-generation firewalls.

Our 24/7 SOC leverages SIEM/XDR and UEBA for real-time threat hunting. Incident response playbooks are tested via tabletop exercises, refined through root-cause analysis and continuous Red/Purple Team engagements. All controls map to POPIA, GDPR, and ISO 27001 compliance, with executive dashboards and quarterly reviews ensuring transparency.

Core Offerings

  • Firewall & Perimeter Security: NGFW & network segmentation
  • Endpoint Protection: EDR for desktops, servers & mobile
  • Threat Detection & Response: SIEM/XDR & behavioral analytics
  • Vulnerability Management: Automated scanning & pentesting
  • Compliance Advisory: POPIA, GDPR & ISO 27001 readiness
