Our Approach

We start with a Cloud Readiness Assessment to evaluate your current environment and workload suitability. Our architects then design a tailored cloud strategy—public, private, or hybrid—focusing on security, cost optimization, and performance. We handle migration planning, data transfer, and application refactoring using automated IaC templates and CI/CD pipelines.

Post–migration, we implement monitoring, backup, and disaster recovery solutions, ensuring SLA-aligned operations and continuous optimization through usage analytics and cost governance.

Core Offerings

  • Cloud Migration: Lift-and-shift, re-platforming, and re-architecting.
  • Infrastructure as Code: Automated deployments with Terraform & Ansible.
  • Cost Management: Budget controls, reserved instances, and rightsizing.
  • Disaster Recovery & Backup: Multi-region replication & retention policies.
